## Prompt Nano Banana pour le kid

```
Cute cartoon illustration of a happy 9-year-old child mid-air in a dynamic
parkour move (horizontal flying leap or wall-run jump). Gender-neutral
appearance, light tan skin, short tousled brown hair blown by the motion,
joyful confident expression with a small smile, eyes looking forward.

Outfit: bright athletic summer clothes — fitted electric blue (#006AFF)
t-shirt with a small white stencil-style "PKBA" detail on the chest,
warm yellow (#FFD23F) athletic shorts, clean white sneakers.

Style: modern flat cartoon illustration, bold clean line work, simple
flat colors with one soft shadow layer, slightly chunky stylized
proportions (big head, expressive limbs). Inspired by contemporary
children's book illustration and Dribbble-style flat art. NOT
photorealistic, NOT 3D rendered, NOT anime, NOT painterly.

Pose: full body visible, strong diagonal composition, leaning forward,
arms outstretched for balance, one leg tucked, the other extending — the
character should feel weightless and flying through the air with
confidence and joy. Add subtle minimal motion lines (3-4 short curved
white strokes) trailing behind the limbs to suggest speed.

Background: pure solid white (#FFFFFF) background, completely empty, no
environment, no ground, no shadow cast on any surface. The character is
isolated so it can be cut out cleanly.

Color palette restricted to: electric blue #006AFF, warm yellow #FFD23F,
coral red #FF6B6B (accents only on shoe stripes or wristband), natural
skin tones, white, soft dark navy #0A1F4D for outlines.

Format: square 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution, character centered
with ~10% breathing room on all sides.

Avoid: text, logos other than the small PKBA chest detail, weapons,
fear/anxiety expressions, dark moody tones, cluttered details,
background scenery, ground shadow, photorealism, hyperrealistic textures.
```

→ Générer 4 variantes, choisir la plus dynamique et "parent-friendly", détourer (remove.bg ou Canva BG Remover), sauvegarder dans `assets/kid-parkour.png`.
